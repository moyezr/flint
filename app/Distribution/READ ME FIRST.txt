FLINT PUBLIC BETA — READ BEFORE OPENING
=======================================

Flint is currently an independent, unnotarized public beta.

The first time you open Flint, macOS will display a “Flint Not Opened” warning
because Apple cannot verify the developer. This warning does not mean macOS
found malware. It means this beta has not been signed and notarized through
Apple's paid developer program.

HOW TO OPEN FLINT

1. Drag Flint into the Applications folder.
2. Open Flint once. When “Flint Not Opened” appears, click Done. Do not move
   Flint to Trash.
3. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security.
4. Scroll to the Security section and click Open Anyway for Flint.
5. Enter your Mac password and confirm Open.
6. Flint will then request Microphone, Accessibility, and Input Monitoring
   permissions for dictation, text insertion, and the global shortcut.

Apple makes Open Anyway available for a limited time after the blocked launch
attempt. If it is missing, try opening Flint once more and return to Privacy &
Security.

Flint processes dictation on your Mac. For the complete install guide, privacy
policy, release checksum, and support contact, visit:

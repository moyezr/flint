# Landing Page Asset Brief

The prototype uses code-native visual placeholders. Replace them with production media before launch without changing the layout or copy.

## 1. Flint Command Surface

- Placement: hero visual, desktop and mobile.
- Format: PNG or WebP, 16:10 minimum 2400 px wide.
- Subject: the actual compact Flint dictation indicator over a real writing environment, showing the live meter but no readable personal text.
- Treatment: charcoal/graphite interface, controlled orange Flint accent, high contrast, crisp edges.
- Avoid: blurred screens, stock desk scenes, glassmorphism, gradients, oversized overlay, identifiable customer data.
- Accessibility alt: "Flint dictation indicator while recording on a Mac."

## 2. Cursor Workflow Photograph

- Placement: workflow proof section.
- Format: PNG or WebP, 4:3 minimum 1800 px wide.
- Subject: a genuine Mac workflow with Flint dictating into a document or chat composer, photographed or captured with the typed content unreadable.
- Treatment: bright neutral environment, real product state visible, quiet composition with generous negative space.
- Avoid: generic microphone imagery, dark blurred laptops, visible private messages, captions baked into the image.
- Accessibility alt: "Flint placing dictated text in an active writing field on a Mac."

## 3. Social Share Card

- Placement: Open Graph and social previews.
- Format: 1200 x 630 PNG.
- Subject: Flint wordmark, the compact indicator, and the phrase "Local Dictation for Mac".
- Treatment: the same graphite, warm white, and orange palette as the product; sharp geometry; no gradients.
- Avoid: platform logos, dense copy, screenshots with unreadable tiny text.

## 4. Favicon and Browser Icons

- Placement: browser tabs, pinned tabs, installable web metadata.
- Format: SVG source plus 512 x 512 PNG and 180 x 180 PNG exports.
- Subject: the Flint microphone/spark mark only, centered with generous padding.
- Treatment: legible at 16 px and monochrome-safe.

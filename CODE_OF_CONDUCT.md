# Code of conduct

Flint's community should be a respectful place to build and discuss software.

Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- Discuss ideas and disagreements constructively.
- Critique work without attacking the person behind it.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's private information.
- Welcome people regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.
- Stop behavior that a maintainer identifies as disruptive or unsafe.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, sustained
disruption, and deliberate exposure of private information are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to `moyezrabbani.work@gmail.com`. Reports will
be reviewed as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may edit or remove
content, close contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community.
